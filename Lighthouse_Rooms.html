Three ways to stay at Lighthouse

Every cabana sits some distance from the next, spaced across the property so each stay feels private. Here's a closer look at all three room types.

+6 more
Ground level

Cabanas

Private, ground-level cabanas with a small front porch — the simplest way to stay at Lighthouse, and a favourite with guests who just want a comfortable, quiet base close to the sand.

  • Single, double, or triple configurations
  • Private ensuite bathroom
  • Small front porch with seating
  • Ceiling fan, mosquito netting, solar-powered lighting

Elevated

Treehouse Cabanas

Raised up among the trees, these rooms trade the ground-level porch for an open-air view over the property and out towards the bay — a favourite for longer sunset evenings.

  • Single, double, or triple configurations
  • Elevated, open-air outlook
  • Private ensuite bathroom
  • Quietest rooms on the property

Family Cabana

Set above the restaurant, this larger room sleeps 6–8 — built for families and groups who want to stay together under one roof, without giving up privacy from the rest of the property.
