About

Space, quiet, and a lighter footprint

Nestled at Lighthouse Point near Komari Lagoon, on a vast stretch of beachfront surrounded by small farms and lush foliage. A 30-minute drive from Arugam Bay keeps the surf season's energy within reach, while the property itself stays calm. Just eight cabanas sit on the property, spaced well apart for privacy, and built mostly from natural materials. Solar panels generate the power, and meals are prepared from produce grown right on the on-site organic farm.

Things to do

Quiet coastline, close by

low tide

Lighthouse Point & Green Point

A right-hand break ideal for intermediate surfers between mid-July and September, once a closely guarded local secret. Green Point is a 15-minute walk north.

mid tide

Pottuvil Lagoon Safari

Mangrove-fed lagoon tours run by local fishermen, home to crocodiles, peacocks, pelicans, and the occasional elephant.

high tide

Muhudu Maha Viharaya

The remains of a shrine dating back over 2,000 years, tucked behind Pottuvil — still holding some of its old mystique.

full day

Jungle Safari

A day trip into nearby jungle and national park territory — keep an eye out for elephants, water buffalo, and a wide range of birdlife, best arranged through the property.
